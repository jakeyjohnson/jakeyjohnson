The Concept

Padel is the fastest growing sport in the UK.
We made it a night out.

PartyPadel.UK brings together short-format padel leagues, iconic warehouse venues and proper DJ-led entertainment — played out in front of a crowd. Turn up solo or with mates, get matched into games, play some padel, then stay for the show. No club membership. Just padel, people and a party.

Short Format Leagues

Round-robin format so you play with and against loads of new people. All skill levels mixed and matched fairly.

Live DJs & Entertainment

Every event runs courtside with resident DJs, lighting rigs and a proper afterparty.

Big Crowd Atmosphere

Play behind the glass in front of a crowd — iconic warehouse venues built for a show, not just a booking.

Compete. Win. Celebrate.

Real matches, real prizes, then rackets down and the party takes over.
