The Concept

Padel is the fastest growing sport in the UK.
We made it a night out.

PartyPadel.UK brings together social round-robin padel tournaments, top venues and proper DJ-led afterparties. Turn up solo or with mates, get matched into games, play some padel, then stay for drinks and music. No club membership. No rankings. Just padel, people and a party.
