The Format

20 minutes. No hiding.

Short-format doubles built for a full night of fixtures, not an afternoon of waiting around.

01

Enter

Sign up individually into a division. Rate your level, one entry.

02

Warm Up

Courtside check-in, walk-on, warm-up rally before the clock starts.

03

Rotate

New partner, new opponents, every round. Every point counts toward your own score.

04

Finals

Top individual scorers progress to a feature-court final under full production.
