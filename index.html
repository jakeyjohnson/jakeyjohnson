What's On

Three days, one very good reason to be hungry.

Every ticket includes the tasting hall, live demo stage and entertainment — food, drink and the cheese itself are pay-as-you-go from the stalls.

01

Live cutting & carving

Watch giant truckles cracked open on the hour, every hour, by the makers themselves.

02

Cheese & cider pairing hall

Guided tastings pairing West Country cheddars with cider and craft beer from local breweries.

03

The British Cheese Awards

See — and taste — the winning wheels before anyone else, judged live on the Saturday.

04

Meet the makers

120+ stalls from small-batch dairies across the UK — talk to the people who actually made it.

05

Live music

Folk and brass on the main stage all weekend, plus a kids' corner with cheese-making workshops.

06

Meet the herd

A paddock of the actual cows, goats and sheep behind your favourite stalls — always a hit with kids.
