Both days open with the farmers market at 10:00. After that, Saturday and Sunday go their separate ways entirely.

Market 10:00 · Main event 14:00–21:30

  • Live music — legacy and chart-topping bands
  • Cooking demos all afternoon
  • Party late into the evening

Market 10:00 · Main event 14:00–21:30
