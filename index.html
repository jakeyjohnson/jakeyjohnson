Savour the flavours of Britain.

Saturday 5 June

The family day.

Gates and the farmers market open at ten. From two, the main arena fills up: funfair rides, fete games, cooking demos through the afternoon and live music from legacy and chart-topping bands, running late into the evening.

Saturday tickets
Sunday 6 June

The Proms.

A quieter, more traditional turn. The market runs as usual in the morning, then a full philharmonic orchestra takes the main stage with leading singers into the evening, finishing on a grand spectacle.

Sunday tickets
The market

250 British cheeses, and the makers behind every one.

Territorials, soft and blue, washed rinds, goat and sheep — from dairies across Britain, each with someone on the stall who can tell you exactly how it was made.
