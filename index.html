The Event

Two very different days.

Both days open with a farmers market from 10:00–13:30. From there, Saturday and Sunday take on a character all their own.

SAT

14:00–21:30 — Family Day

Funfairs, village fete-style activations, live music from legacy and current chart-topping bands, cooking demos, and a party late into the evening.

SUN

14:00–21:30 — The Proms

A far more traditional, proms-styled event with a philharmonic orchestra performing alongside leading singers, finishing with a grand spectacle.

01

Artisan cheese & produce

Sampling zones with traditional and contemporary British cheeses, breads, chutneys and drinks.

02

Interactive workshops

Live cheesemaking demos, pairing sessions and culinary masterclasses for all ages.

03

Family activities

Mini-farm experiences, games, storytelling and children's tasting areas.

04

Market-style stalls

60+ artisanal vendors selling cheese, preserves, drinks and handcrafted goods.

Saturday 16 May

A proper village fete, scaled up.

Funfairs, fete-style activations, live bands and cooking demos — running from the farmers market at 10:00 through to a party late into the evening.
