Format

20 Minutes. No Hiding.

Adapted Americano — you sign up solo, we rotate partners and opponents all night. Built for constant play, not an afternoon of waiting around for your next match.

01

Enter

Sign up individually into Beginners or Advanced ahead of the event. Rate your level, one entry, confirmation by email.

02

Warm Up

Courtside check-in and a warm-up rally before the clock starts on your first round.

03

Rotate

New partner, new opponents, every round — Americano scoring means every point you win counts toward your own total.

04

Finals

Top individual scorers from each division progress to a feature-court final, under full DJ and lighting production.
