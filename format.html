Format

20 Minutes. No Hiding.

Short-format doubles, built for a full night of fixtures — not an afternoon of waiting around for your next match.

01

Enter

Register your team into a division ahead of the event. Two players, one entry, confirmation by email.

02

Warm Up

Courtside check-in, team walk-on, and a warm-up rally before the clock starts on your first fixture.

03

Compete

Short-format group matches — every team is guaranteed a full round of fixtures regardless of result.

04

Finals

Top teams from each division progress to a feature-court final, under full DJ and lighting production.
