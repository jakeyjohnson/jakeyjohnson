About

Serious Sport.
Social Energy.

Party Padel turns competitive padel into a one-night, high-energy live event. Quick-format doubles, a cash prize for the winning team, DJs, lighting, walk-ons, social spaces and finals production combine the accessibility of club padel with the atmosphere of a major sporting event.

Not a club. Not a tournament. Not a nightclub with courts.

Party Padel sits between competitive social sport and live entertainment. Every player should feel like an athlete. Every spectator should feel like they're at an event worth attending.

Who it's for

Socially active 20–40 year olds who already play padel, racket sports, gym or social sport — and the friends, partners and spectators who come for the music, the competition and the night out.

North Star

If a decision makes Party Padel look like a normal padel club, we remove it. If it makes the competition feel fake or secondary to the party, we remove it. The identity works when both sides feel equally credible.
