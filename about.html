Our story

Started by a handful of West Country cheesemakers who wanted somewhere to show off what they'd made — now Britain's biggest cheese festival.

Why we started

Small dairies rarely get a stage. Supermarket shelves favour the big names, and most people never get to talk to the person who actually made their cheese. We wanted a weekend built entirely around changing that — putting independent producers front and centre, with room to talk, taste and sell directly to the people who love what they make.

What makes it different

No resellers, no supermarket own-brands — every stall is the producer themselves. That means better prices for them, better stories for you, and cheese you genuinely can't buy anywhere else.
