Address

Ashmere Park

Northamptonshire, NN6 0AA (illustrative). Follow the yellow AA signs from the A508, not your sat-nav's shortcut.

Getting here

Come by coach and pay £40 less

Two thirds of our footprint is people travelling, so the coach is the cheapest ticket we sell, not an afterthought. Fourteen cities, two pickups each, bikes in the hold, and a driver who has done this run before.

By train: Market Harborough is the nearest station, with a shuttle bus every 20 minutes from Thursday 11am to Monday noon, included in every ticket. By bike: a staffed, fenced compound plus a free breakfast on Friday for anyone who cycled more than 20 miles.

By car: parking is included in adult tickets but please fill the seats — a single-occupancy car is the most expensive way to get here in every sense. Campervans need a separate pass and there are 400 of them.

Accessibility

Tell us what you need and we'll sort it

Essential companion tickets are free. The access team reads every application personally — there is no form robot and no need to prove anything twice.

Accessible camping

Level, hard-standing pitches next to accessible loos and showers, a five-minute roll from the Main Meadow. Powerchair charging on site.

Viewing platforms

At the Main Meadow, The Hollow and Cantina Carnivale, each with a companion space and a BSL-interpreted set every day.

Quiet spaces

Two low-sensory tents, ear defenders to borrow at the info point, and a printed sensory map of the loudest parts of site.

Email the access team

What to bring

Pack light, pack warm

A Northamptonshire valley in late August can do 28°C and a thunderstorm in the same afternoon. Plan for both and you'll be the most popular person in your camp.

Do bring

  • Refillable bottle — water is free everywhere
  • Wellies and a proper waterproof
  • A warm layer for 3am
  • Sun cream and a hat
  • Fancy dress. Saturday is not a suggestion
  • A torch, and spare batteries

Please don't

  • Single-use plastic — we'll take it off you
  • Glass, anywhere on site
  • Disposable BBQs or fire pits
  • Chinese lanterns, flares, fireworks
  • Nitrous oxide canisters
  • Drones, unless you're accredited
