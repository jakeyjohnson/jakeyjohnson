Line-up 2027

Wave one of three. Around 200 acts across 14 stages, plus a stubborn fifth of the programme we never announce — because finding it yourself is the entire point.

Beyond the bands

Roughly half of what happens isn't music

Fourteen stages covers the ones with a PA. It doesn't cover the sauna, the debates, the Olympics, or the man who has run a tea shop out of a converted horsebox every year since 2011.

The Wilderwood Olympics

Welly wanging, sedan-chair racing, competitive napping. Heats on Saturday, final on Sunday, medals struck from last year's scaffold clamps.

The Long Table

Debates, talks and arguments about land, water, energy and who gets to decide. Chaired badly and on purpose.

Workshops

Willow weaving, bread, harmony singing, bike repair, scything. Around 120 sessions, all free, most first-come.

Little Sprouts

A whole field for under-12s and the adults attached to them. Dens, mud, tiny discos, and a quiet tent that saves lives.
