Come and build it

About a third of Wilderwood is made by people who first turned up as punters. Volunteer, perform, trade, crew, or bring an idea so odd we have to say yes.

Volunteer

Three shifts, a free ticket, the best seat in the house

We take on 600 volunteers a year across stewarding, bars, litter, info points, the kids' field and the recycling crew. Three six-hour shifts across the weekend, arranged around the acts you actually want to see.

You get a free ticket, crew catering three times a day, a dedicated quiet campsite, showers that aren't rammed, and a deposit back in full when your last shift is signed off.

Apply to volunteer

  1. Apply by 30 April

    Ten minutes. Tell us what you're good at and when you can arrive.

  2. £60 deposit

    Refunded in full once your final shift is signed off. It's the only thing that makes rotas work.

  3. Pick your shifts

    Rotas open in June. First come, first served, and yes you can swap.

  4. Arrive Wednesday

    A day early, for the briefing, the crew barbecue (also meat-free) and the good camping spots.

Perform

We book 200 acts and half come through the form

Applications for 2027 close 31 January. We listen to every single one, in a room, with tea, over about three weeks in February. Yes, it takes ages. No, we're not going to stop.

Bands & solo

Anything with a pulse. Send one link, not twelve.

DJs

The Hollow and Cantina. A mix and a rough idea of the floor you'd fill.

Walkabout & circus

Stilts, brass, puppetry, a man dressed as a heron. Especially the heron.

Words & ideas

Poets, comics, speakers and anyone who can chair a debate without shouting.

Trade with us

Independent, mostly local, entirely plant-based

Forty food pitches and sixty market stalls. We favour small operators within 100 miles, we cap the number of any one cuisine, and we don't take chains at any price.

Pitch requirements

What we ask of traders

  • Fully vegetarian menu, clearly allergen-labelled
  • No single-use plastic, at all, including straws
  • Compostable or reusable serveware only
  • Renewable power or our site supply — no private generators
  • At least one item under £7

Crew & build

Paid work, three weeks either side

Build starts mid-August and breakdown finishes in September. Scaffolders, riggers, electricians, drivers, carpenters, site plumbers, welfare, medics and a decorations crew that works largely in sequins.

Site build

Fencing, staging, structures, roads, water. Experience welcome, enthusiasm considered.

Production

Sound, lights, stage management, backline. Fourteen stages need fourteen crews.

Welfare & medical

Qualified first aiders, mental health first aiders, safer-spaces and night welfare.
