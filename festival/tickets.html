Payment plan

£45 now, the rest over four months

Every ticket type can be split. Pay a £45 deposit, then four equal instalments taken on the 1st of the month from April. No interest, no credit check and a £3 admin fee that goes to the coach subsidy.

Miss a payment and we email you twice before anything happens. Cancel before 1 July and you get everything back bar the deposit.

  4. Tickets in August

Green Traveller

The cheapest ticket is the one that leaves the car at home

Getting here is two thirds of this festival's carbon footprint, and no amount of solar panels on the main stage touches that. So we made the low-carbon option the cheapest one, rather than a badge you get for feeling virtuous.

Coaches run from London, Bristol, Manchester, Leeds, Glasgow, Cardiff, Norwich, Birmingham, Newcastle, Sheffield, Brighton, Nottingham, Liverpool and Edinburgh. Two pickups each, bikes in the hold, and a shuttle from Market Harborough station every 20 minutes.
