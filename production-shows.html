What Is A Production Show?

Multi-skilled performance, built to one brief

A Production Show pulls together several disciplines — fire, aerial, dance, roller-skating, live music — and choreographs them into a single narrative moment, timed to your event rather than slotted between other suppliers. It's the difference between booking three acts and commissioning one show.

Every show starts from nothing: no off-the-shelf routines. We design around your venue, your guest list and the moment you want to create.
