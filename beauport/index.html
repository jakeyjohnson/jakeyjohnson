What we do

Three things, done properly.

Corporate

Board meetings, AGMs and LP visits handled end to end — travel, venues, hospitality and the week around them — for funds, trustees and family offices based in or visiting Jersey.

Private clients

One point of contact for travel, property, education, household staff and the everyday logistics of a life split between Jersey and two or three other cities.

Access

Restaurants, events, private views and introductions — arranged quietly, and only ever things we can genuinely deliver.

The difference

Velocity Black gets you the table.
Beauport is at it.

App-based concierges are excellent at transactions — a reservation, an upgrade, a ticket. Beauport is built for the things that need a person on the ground: meeting a relocating family off the plane, walking a property before you view it remotely, standing in for you at a handover when you can't be on-island yourself.
