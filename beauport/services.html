Services

Everything handled directly, by someone you can call by name.

Beauport works across three areas. Most clients use all three at different points in the year.

01

Corporate

Board meetings, AGMs, LP visits and staff relocation, run by people who know Jersey's calendar and venues from the inside.

  • Board and AGM logistics — venues, travel, catering and the evening around the meeting
  • LP and investor visits — itineraries, transport and hospitality for the whole trip, not just the meeting room
  • Executive relocation to Jersey — property search, schools, and settling-in support run alongside your trust or legal team, not instead of them
  • Off-island offsites in London, Geneva and Paris

02

Private clients

One point of contact for a life that moves between Jersey and two or three other cities.

  • Travel — private and scheduled, coordinated end to end
  • Property — viewings, handovers, and standing in for you when you can't be on-island
  • Household — staff sourcing and management liaison, working with your existing advisers
  • Family — school places, medical appointments, and the logistics around them

03

Access

Arranged quietly, and only ever things we can genuinely deliver.

  • Restaurant tables and private dining, in Jersey and off it
  • Events, private views and members' clubs
  • Personal shopping and gifting, sourced in person
  • Tickets and introductions where we have a genuine, direct relationship — never a promise we can't keep

Not sure which of these you need? That's normal — most requests touch more than one.
