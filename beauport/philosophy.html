Philosophy

Why in person, and why Jersey.

Jersey runs on a model the rest of the concierge industry has largely abandoned: a small number of named people, personally accountable, who you can reach directly. It's how the island's trust and funds industry has worked for decades. Beauport exists because nobody had built the same model for the parts of a client's life that sit outside a trust deed.

The app-concierge problem

A chat window and a global partner network is genuinely good at transactional requests — a reservation, an upgrade, a ticket. It's weaker at anything that requires judgement on the ground: knowing which property actually suits a family before they've seen it, standing in for a principal at a handover, or noticing that an AGM week needs a second car because two LPs are flying in on different days. Those calls are made by someone who has walked the site, not read a brief.

What "in person" actually means here

  • A resident team based on Jersey, not a call centre routing to local partners
  • The same one or two people for every request, not a rotating queue
  • Account managers who travel to London, Geneva and Paris with clients, rather than handing off to an unfamiliar local contact
  • A willingness to say no to a request we can't do properly, rather than outsourcing it and hoping

Why Jersey specifically

Jersey's private wealth and funds industry means an unusually high concentration of principals, trustees and fund managers already operate to a standard of discretion most cities don't ask for. Beauport starts here because the client base and the operating culture already exist — the island was simply missing the layer that handles everything outside the trust structure.

"We'd rather do three things properly than fifty adequately."
