Privacy & Cookies

What we collect, why, and how to change your mind. Last updated 8 August 2026.

Cookies we use

We keep this to the minimum needed to run the site. Nothing here tracks you across other websites, and nothing is sold to third parties.

NamePurposeTypeDuration
pp_cookie_consentRemembers your cookie choice so we don't ask every visitStrictly necessaryUntil cleared

Strictly necessary storage like this doesn't legally require consent, and it's the only thing this site currently sets. If we add analytics or advertising cookies in future — to understand traffic or measure a campaign — we'll list them here and ask again before they're set. Nothing optional runs until you say yes.

Third-party requests

Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which involves your browser making a direct request to Google and sharing your IP address as a normal part of that request. Google Fonts does not set cookies for static font files. No other third-party scripts run on this site.

Data you give us directly

When you enter a team or make a partner enquiry, we collect the details you submit — team/player names, contact email, and payment information handled by our payment provider (we never see or store full card details). We use this to run the event you've entered: confirmations, schedule updates, and event-day organisation. We don't share it with anyone outside what's needed to run the event and process payment.

Your choices

  • Change your cookie preference any time using the button below.
  • Ask what data we hold on you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing hello@partypadel.uk.
  • Clearing your browser's site data resets your cookie choice, and you'll be asked again next visit.
